About Me

Dedicated robotics engineer seeking advanced research opportunities

I am a Robotics Engineer with a strong foundation in autonomous systems, embedded programming, and robotic reinforcement learning. My academic journey has been driven by curiosity and a passion for solving real-world problems through intelligent machines.

Through hands-on projects, research internships, and collaborative work, I have developed expertise in ROS/ROS2, computer vision, RL, DL, SLAM, CAD and embedded systems design. My work has been recognized in multiple competitions and has resulted in one published conference paper and provisional patent.

I am now deepening my research in autonomous navigation, and RL for Robot Learning, with the goal of contributing to cutting-edge robotics research.
