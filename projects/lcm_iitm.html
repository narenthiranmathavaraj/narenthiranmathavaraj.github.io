LCM Device for Surgeon Training in Minimally Invasive Surgery

ROS1 Embedded C Python Fusion 360
▶

LCM Device teleoperating Patient Side Manipulator

📝 Executive Summary

Designed and fabricated a Low-Cost Master [LCM] device that will be used to control the end effector of KUKA iiwa (the slave device) that maintains Remote Center-of-Motion [RCM] for minimally invasive surgery via teleoperation.

Tested the controls of the LCM in Rviz and Gazebo. Also designed and worked on controls of an updated in-house Low-Cost Master Tool Manipulator [MTM] to control in-house Patient Side Manipulator [PSM]

<5ms Latency

🎯 Key Features

  • Total Cost of the training kit: <$2,500
  • Latency: <5 ms (meets medical standards)
  • Sensor Fusion: BNO055 IMU + AS5048B 14–bit Magnetic Encoders + ESP32.
