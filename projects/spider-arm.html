Spider Arm Hardware Assembly [Pick and Place]

📝 Executive Summary

Spider Arm is a 6-DOF serial manipulator designed for cost-effective automation research. It aims to achieve maximum precision on a budget.

From mechanical design in Fusion 360 to motion planning in MoveIt, this project covers the full vertical of robotic arm development, including real-world fabrication and control via Teensy 4.1.

6 Degrees of Freedom

🎯 Key Features

  • Extended Reach: 700mm workspace with ±5mm precision.
  • Cost-Effective: Sub-$700 budget vs industrial arms ($20k+).
  • Motion Planning: Integrated with ROS MoveIt for trajectory generation.
  • Fabrication: Phase-I 4-DOF hardware assembly tested successfully.
